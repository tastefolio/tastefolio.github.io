Cities race to cool their streets as summers lengthen

From tree canopies to white roofs, municipalities are running experiments that take up to five degrees off the pavement.

By Sara Mohammadi · 12 min read

When sidewalk thermometers touched 56 degrees last month, city officials concluded that shade was no longer a luxury. A new program will plant 12,000 trees on the hottest blocks over the next three years.

Researchers say the combination of trees, lighter surfaces and small water features matters most — though the cost of upkeep is still being debated.
